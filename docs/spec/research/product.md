# Research: product

> Snapshot from 2026-10-14, written by Claude's research agents for the app spec (`../CLAUDE.md`). Each finding lists its sources and a confidence level. Recheck anything time-sensitive (versions, prices, store policies) before acting on it.

## Summary
Voice recitation is no longer a differentiator. As of Oct 2026 several Bible-memory apps score spoken recitation word by word: Bread, ReciteWell (on-device Whisper), Recite by Ocho, Versify, Verses' "Speak Out" beta and Bible Memory's $49.99/yr Unlimited tier. Tarteel does the same for the Quran. None of the surveyed apps teaches an outline before the text or is built for long passages, so Seedling's lasting edge is the sequence-v2 method plus gentle, church-oriented design. "Free" alone is not an edge either, because Remember Me (Swiss nonprofit, open source, free sync and groups), Versify and Recite are free. Competitor reviews point to four things that matter for older or less tech-savvy users: forgiving input (adjacent-key typos accepted), instant haptic feedback, a tutorial, and never landing on a paywall from a reminder. The most common voice complaint is correct recitation being marked wrong. Grading should be automatic, because self-ratings are unreliable: Karpicke & Roediger (2008) found learners' predictions uncorrelated with their actual recall, and Kornell & Bjork (2009) and Dunlosky & Rawson (2012) document overconfidence. Anki's FSRS FAQ says Again/Good-only grading works and "may even be more accurate", so the app can map voice/typing/hint signals to mostly binary ratings with no four-button screen. On motivation, intact streaks raise engagement but broken ones cause drop-off, an effect weaker when a streak can be repaired (Silverman & Barasch 2023, JCR). Habitica-style punishment produced counterproductive effects in every participant of a 45-person field study (Diefenbach & Müssig 2019). Seedling should therefore use a weekly rhythm with built-in rest days and repair, and a plant that never dies. The plant model I propose is a deterministic, outline-driven tree: outline sections become branches that grow during Phase 1, verses become leaves whose stage follows FSRS stability (rooted at 21 days or more), and leaves droop gently when retrievability falls below 0.9. It renders with @shopify/react-native-skia 2.14.0 (needs Reanimated 4). The group tree is 66 book branches built from per-member mastered-verse bitsets of about 3.9 KB (BSB: 66 books, 1,189 chapters, 31,086 verses).

- **[high]** Anki's official FSRS FAQ says FSRS works fine with only Again and Good, and 'may even be more accurate'. FSRS ignores time spent on a card and uses only interval lengths and grades. Since Anki 24.06.3 the optimizer runs on any number of reviews.
  - FSRS developers on the Anki forum add that FSRS hard-codes Hard, Good and Easy as passes and Again as the only fail. Users who press Hard when they forgot an answer distort their schedules, and the fix has to come from the interface, not the algorithm.
  - sources: https://docs.ankiweb.net/faqs/frequently-asked-questions-about-fsrs
- **[high]** ts-fsrs is at 5.4.2 on npm (MIT, Node 20 or newer) and implements FSRS-6 (21 parameters). The API includes fsrs(), createEmptyCard(), repeat() (previews all four ratings), next(card, now, rating), get_retrievability, forgetting_curve, rollback, forget and reschedule. Options include request_retention, maximum_interval, enable_fuzz, enable_short_term, learning_steps and relearning_steps.
  - sources: https://registry.npmjs.org/ts-fsrs/latest, https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs, https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs/blob/main/packages/fsrs/README.md
- **[high]** FSRS-6 forgetting curve: R(t,S) = (1 + factor·t/S)^(−w20), where factor = 0.9^(−1/w20) − 1, so R equals 0.9 when t = S. Stability S is the interval at which recall probability falls to 90%. This gives the plant a principled 'needs water' signal: R < 0.9 means overdue.
  - sources: https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/awesome-fsrs/wiki/The-Algorithm
- **[high]** Self-ratings are unreliable, which supports automatic grading. In Karpicke & Roediger (2008, Science), students' predictions of their performance were uncorrelated with their actual recall. Kornell & Bjork (2009, JEP: General) found a 'stability bias': people underestimate both future learning and future forgetting. Dunlosky & Rawson (2012, Learning & Instruction 22:271–280) found overconfident self-evaluation leads to underachievement, and that scoring yourself against the correct answer's idea units improves accuracy.
  - Design implication: when grading has to fall back to the user, show the text and have them tap the words they missed (scoring against a standard) instead of asking how well they did.
  - sources: https://www.brucehayes.org/Teaching/papers/2008_Roediger_Karpicke_Science.pdf, https://web.williams.edu/Psychology/Faculty/Kornell/Publications/Kornell.Bjork.2009.pdf, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2011.08.003
- **[medium]** Hesitation is only weak evidence. Slower retrieval can mean useful effort or a failed retrieval, and fluency in the moment can differ from long-term strength. Use pauses only to choose Hard over Good, never to trigger Again.
  - sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6289840, https://umimpact.umt.edu/en/publications/item-repetition-and-retrieval-processes-in-cued-recall-analysis-o/
- **[medium]** Speech recognition makes more errors on older voices. Word error rate rises gradually with age (Vipperla, Renals & Frankel 2008, using Supreme Court recordings). A European Portuguese read-speech corpus showed a 41% relative increase from age 60–65 to 81–86. Slower speaking rate is a likely cause.
  - These are pre-transformer systems, so current gaps may be smaller, but the direction is consistent. Implications: lenient matching, long silence timeouts, and a typed or self-check fallback.
  - sources: https://www.cstr.inf.ed.ac.uk/downloads/publications/2008/vipperla_is08.pdf, https://era.ed.ac.uk/handle/1842/5725, https://ciencia.iscte-iul.pt/publications/impact-of-age-in-asr-for-the-elderly-preliminary-experiments-in-european-portuguese/96340?lang=en

## recommendations
- POSITIONING: Market Seedling as the app for learning whole passages (Sermon on the Mount, Farewell Discourse, Psalms), not single verses: 'learn the outline, then the words, then say it'. Voice scoring and 'free' are table stakes now (Bread, ReciteWell, Recite, Versify, Remember Me). Differentiate on: (1) outline-first ladders, (2) gentle design for older users, (3) church group tree, (4) BSB and no tracking. Never put a paywall or donation prompt on a reminder or study path; that is a top Bible Memory complaint.
- AUTO-GRADING (replaces the four buttons). Each recall card records an Attempt {mode: voice|type|selfcheck, wordsTotal, wordsMatched, wordsClose, wordsMissed, longestMissedRun, hintsUsed, peeks, hesitations, durationMs}. Map it to a ts-fsrs Rating (port of trial 2's type-mode rule). Again if a hint or peek was used, 2 or more words were missed, a run of 3 or more consecutive words was missed, or the user tapped 'Show me'. Good if at most 1 word was missed or close, no hints, and fewer than 2 hesitations. Hard otherwise. Easy is assigned automatically only on Read-through and Preview cards (always) and on scaffold cards (Missing words, First letters) at 100% with no hesitations, which retires the scaffolding as sequence v2 §6 intends. After grading, show plain language: 'Well done. See you again in 3 days.' Add a small 'That's not right' link with two options ('I needed more help', 'That was easy'). Log both autoRating and finalRating so the thresholds can be calibrated against trial 2 and beta data.
- VOICE MATCHING: Normalize both texts: lowercase; strip punctuation and quote marks; numerals to words; expand contractions; treat 'Lord/LORD/lord' as equal. Align at the word level with Needleman-Wunsch or Levenshtein. Count a word as 'close' if its double-metaphone code matches (npm double-metaphone 2.0.1) or it is within edit distance 1 for words over 4 letters. Ignore fillers (um, uh) and immediate self-corrections. Pass the passage's proper nouns as contextualStrings. Count a hesitation as a pause over max(3 s, 3× the user's median gap between words), from per-word timestamps where the OS provides them (iOS 17+, Android 14+ on-device). Hesitations may downgrade Good to Hard, never to Again. Make the error asymmetric: a wrong fail hurts motivation more than a wrong pass hurts the schedule, so if most mismatches are proper nouns or STT confidence is low, cap the result at Hard and ask 'Did you say it right?'
- FALLBACKS FOR NON-VOICE USERS (speech differences, noisy rooms, no mic permission): (a) type first letters (Bible Memory style), with adjacent-key forgiveness and a haptic plus visual cue on error; (b) 'Self-check': recite, reveal the text, tap the words you missed, and the app computes the rating. The second is the idea-unit self-scoring that Dunlosky & Rawson found accurate, and it keeps users away from abstract buttons. Default order: voice, then type, then self-check, switchable on every card.
- PLANT GROWTH MODEL v1, a pure, deterministic function: growPlant(outlineTree, cardStates, plantSeed, now, renderVersion) returns {segments[], leaves[], blossoms[]}. Topology is the outline itself: the trunk is the journey root, each section a branch, each sub-section a sub-branch, each unit a twig, and each verse a leaf. Branch length = parentLength × 0.8 × sqrt(wordsInSubtree / wordsInParent). Width follows Leonardo's rule (parent width² = sum of child widths²). Base branch angle is drawn once per plant from U(22°, 38°), with ±6° jitter per node, alternating sides by sibling index. Leaves sit along twigs at seeded alternate or golden-angle (137.5°) positions. Outline mastery (Phase 1) grows the branches: each branch is drawn to growth × length, where growth is 0 before its Preview, 0.35 after the Preview, and then min(1, 0.35 + 0.65 × ln(1+S)/ln(1+7)) using the weaker of its Outline cloze or List-the-parts stability. Text mastery (Phase 2) grows the leaves, using the stability S of the unit's Header→text card. Bud: any card covering the verse has been introduced. Small leaf: Header→text has graduated. Full leaf: S ≥ 7 days. Rooted: S ≥ 21 days (Anki 'mature'). Blossom on a section once its Section header→text card, or all its Missing section cards, reach S ≥ 21 days. Fruit at the crown when the whole journey is rooted. The structure is monotonic: nothing ever shrinks, falls off or dies.
- VITALITY (the gentle droop): for each leaf, R = FSRS-6 retrievability of the card that governs it, computed now. R ≥ 0.9: upright and full color. Below 0.9: droop = 30° × min(1, (0.9 − R)/0.3) and saturation drops by up to 35%. Clamp it there; no browning, no falling. A review 'waters' the leaf: a brief rain-sparkle animation that becomes a crossfade under Reduce Motion. Show the count in neutral words: '6 leaves are thirsty: water them (about 4 min)'. Never use red numbers or say 'overdue'.
- DETERMINISM AND STABLE CHARACTER: create a 128-bit plantSeed when the user starts a journey, store it locally and sync it. For every node, derive a separate seed = hash(plantSeed, nodeId) (xmur3 or FNV-1a) feeding an sfc32 or splitmix32 PRNG, so editing or adding one section never reshapes the rest of the tree. Write the PRNG and hash inline (about 20 lines) instead of depending on seedrandom, which was last published in 2019. Seed-drawn character traits: branch angle, curvature or gnarl, leaf shape (olive, fig, vine leaf, mustard; pick per journey or genre, e.g. Psalms = vine), and blossom palette. Store renderVersion with the plant so later algorithm changes never silently reshape a user's tree.
- RENDERING with @shopify/react-native-skia (install via npx expo install; currently 2.14.0 with Reanimated 4): draw branches as filled tapered Paths and animate growth through Path end trim driven by a shared value. Batch leaves with Atlas or a recorded Picture: the Sermon on the Mount has about 110 leaves, Isaiah 40–44 has 141, Psalms has 2,461. Cache the geometry and recompute only when card states change. For the home-screen widget, export a PNG snapshot from the app and show it in an expo-widgets SwiftUI Image (needs a development build). Give the plant an accessibilityLabel summary, e.g. 'Sermon on the Mount tree: 4 of 5 branches grown, 42 of 111 verses rooted, 6 leaves thirsty'.
- GROUP 'TREE OF THE WHOLE BIBLE': trunk → 10 genre limbs (Law 5, History 12, Wisdom 5, Major Prophets 5, Minor Prophets 12; Gospels 4, Acts 1, Pauline Letters 13, General Letters 8, Revelation 1) → 66 book branches, length ∝ sqrt(verse count) → 1,189 chapter twigs → leaf density = share of the chapter's verses rooted by at least one member. Blossom when 3 or more members (configurable) have the chapter rooted. Privacy-preserving data: each opted-in member syncs only a bitset of rooted verses (31,086 bits, about 3.9 KB) plus a version. The server stores per-group counts per verse. No review logs, ratings or audio leave the device. In Matthew 13:31–32 the mustard seed grows into a tree where birds nest, so show members as small birds, never as a ranked list.
- MOTIVATION WITHOUT SHAME: (1) Replace a daily streak with a weekly 'rhythm' ('4 of 5 days this week'). The user picks a goal of 3–7 days and optional rest days (e.g., Sunday) that never count against them. A missed day can be repaired by studying within the next 2 days (repair blunts post-break drop-off, per Silverman & Barasch). (2) No leaderboards, points or rankings; growth and group birds are informational feedback. ## gotchas
- Third-party review sites in this niche (learnofchrist.com, marlvel.ai, faith.tools, the remem.me comparison pages) are often AI-written, outdated or written by a competitor, and they contradict each other: learnofchrist says Remember Me has no sync or groups, while remem.me says it has both. Rely on store listings and official pages.
- FSRS has no idea that the ladder cards overlap: one verse appears on 6 or more cards. Leaf state must therefore come from one governing card per verse (the unit's Header→text), not an average over all cards, or leaves will flicker as scaffold cards fall due.
- Pressing Hard after forgetting is the classic FSRS misuse. Auto-grading must never output Hard for a failed recall; anything failed is Again.
- Seeding a single sequential PRNG over the whole outline means that adding or renaming one section reshapes the entire tree. Use per-node hashed seeds and a stored renderVersion.
- Drooping leaves can turn into guilt. Cap the droop, never show death or falling leaves, and test the tone with older church members before launch; if testers find it shaming, use 'thirsty' color shifts only.
- Speech recognition is worse for older and slower speakers and for Bible proper nouns (e.g., Zebulun, Naphtali). Verses' 'Speak Out' is the cautionary example of misgrading driving complaints. Grade leniently and offer a non-voice path.
- The sequence-v2 'stable layout' rule (blanks keep their word width) conflicts with Dynamic Type reflow. The layout should stay the same across cards at a given text size, not across sizes. At large sizes the map strip must collapse to a one-line breadcrumb.
- Skia does not run inside iOS widgets (expo-widgets is SwiftUI-only, alpha, and isolated from app state), so the widget plant must be a pre-rendered image.
- Group trees with user-created journeys count as user-generated content under App Store guideline 1.2: they need filtering, reporting, blocking and published contact info. Accounts also require in-app deletion (5.1.1(v)).
- Donations: under App Store rules, only an approved nonprofit can take donations inside the app (3.2.1(vi)). Otherwise the app must stay free and link out to collect (3.2.2(iv)). Never attach a donation prompt to study flows or notifications.
- The react-native-skia docs now show 'latest (v3)' (Graphite) while npm 'latest' is still 2.14.0. Use `npx expo install` and follow the v2 docs unless Expo pins v3.
- Cepeda's 'optimal gap' figures differ between the preprint and the published paper. Cite the published 5–10% of a 1-year delay and 20–40% of a 1-week delay.

## human_actions
- Install and try the main competitors on your iPhone for 15–20 minutes each: Bible Memory (free tier), Remember Me, Recite (Ocho), ReciteWell, Bread, Versify. Ideally sit beside one older church member while they try two of them, and note where they get stuck.
- Recruit 3–5 church members aged 60+ as design testers for the plant tone (is the droop encouraging or shaming?), text sizes, and the voice-grading leniency, before the TestFlight beta.
- Decide with your pastor or elders what group features are appropriate. Should individual progress ever be visible to the group, or only aggregate birds and leaves? Is there a default rest day, such as Sunday?
- Choose the art direction for the plant (leaf species per journey, palette), or approve the use of commissioned or AI-assisted leaf and blossom sprites.
- Confirm whether the church or 501(c)(3) can become an Apple-approved nonprofit for in-app donations, or whether donations must link out to an external page.
- Export your trial 2 (Isaiah 40–44) Anki review log when the trial ends. It is needed to calibrate the auto-grading thresholds and the 'rooted at 21 days' rule.

## open_questions
- Should verse mastery come only from the unit's Header→text card, or should Section header→text (when present) override it as the governing card?
- Should voice grading ever assign Again on its own, or always confirm first ('Looks like you needed help. Try again?') for low-confidence recognitions?
- Should the structure (branches) ever regress if outline cards lapse badly, or stay strictly monotonic with only vitality changing? I recommend monotonic.
- What should 'journeys' be called: Plantings, Gardens, Paths or Passages?
- Should the default 'rhythm' goal be 5 days a week with Sunday as a rest day, or should onboarding ask for it?
- Should a group's 'blossom' threshold (members who know a chapter) scale with group size?
- Should verses the user already knows by heart (e.g., Ps 23) be placeable as already 'rooted' through a quick placement recitation, so long-time memorizers don't start from bare branches?
- Is per-verse plant geometry needed for very large journeys like Psalms (2,461 verses), or should leaves aggregate to chapter level beyond a threshold (e.g., more than 500 verses)?
